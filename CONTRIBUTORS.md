## 🙌 Become a Contributor

Every accepted PR puts you on the wall automatically. Ways to help:

| Contribution | How |
|---|---|
| ➕ Add a free host | Add one row to the right category in [README.md](README.md), in alphabetical order |
| 🔄 Update a free tier | Fix outdated limits and link the provider's pricing page in your PR |
| 🪦 Report a dead service | [Open an issue](https://github.com/nuhmanpk/Awesome-Web-Hosting/issues/new) or check [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) |
| ⭐ Star the repo | Helps more developers find free hosting, and puts you on the Star Wall |
| 📣 Share it | Post the list on X, Reddit, dev.to or your blog |

Start with **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)**.
